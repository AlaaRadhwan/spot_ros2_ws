### Original models and parameters

The URDF/Xacro, `ros2_control` configuration, LiDAR placement, Gazebo worlds' physics and the V3 mass and inertia parameters were written for this project (see the training repository). The warehouse maps in `src/spot_navigation/maps/` were generated by this project's SLAM runs in simulation, but they describe the geometry of the third-party warehouse world below.
